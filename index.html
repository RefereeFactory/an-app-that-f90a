Three Hellos

Hello is the smallest bridge between people. Here it is in three languages — tap any card to hear it spoken aloud, and spread a little warmth.

Audio uses your device's built-in voices.

Say hello your way

Pick any language and hear its greeting. A friendly way to practise before you travel or meet someone new.

Bonjour

Carry the greeting with you

Little printable cards and pocket phrase packs to keep a friendly word close at hand.

How it works

  • Three greeting cards show hello with a simple pronunciation guide.
  • Tap Say it on a card, or Play all three, to hear each greeting using your device's built-in speech voices — no account needed.
  • Use Say hello your way to pick from a wider list and practise any greeting.
  • Everything runs right here in your browser. Audio availability depends on the voices installed on your device.
